What is FOIR? The Bank Secret Formula

FOIR = (All Existing EMIs + New Loan EMI) / Net Monthly Income * 100. Banks in India allow maximum 50-60% FOIR. For example, if your salary is ₹50,000 and you already pay ₹5,000 EMI for bike, bank will allow total EMI up to 55% of salary = ₹27,500. So new personal loan EMI can be max ₹22,500. Based on this EMI, tenure and interest, max loan amount is calculated. Our LoanLens calculator does this reverse calculation automatically. This is why it is more accurate than other fake calculators on Google.
